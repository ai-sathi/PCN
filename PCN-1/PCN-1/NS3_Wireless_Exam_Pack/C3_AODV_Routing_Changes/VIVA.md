# Viva — C3

1. **What is AODV?** Ad hoc On-Demand Distance Vector routing.
2. **Reactive or proactive?** Reactive; it discovers routes when required.
3. **What is a route request?** A control message used to discover a path to a destination.
4. **What is route maintenance?** Detecting broken paths and seeking a usable route.
5. **AODV vs OLSR?** AODV discovers routes on demand; OLSR maintains routes proactively.
6. **Does the supplied minimal topology fully demonstrate multi-hop AODV?** No; use a multi-hop ad hoc topology to evaluate route discovery properly.
