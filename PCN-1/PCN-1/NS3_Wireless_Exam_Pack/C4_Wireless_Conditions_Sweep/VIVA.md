# Viva — C4

1. **What is throughput?** Amount of received data per unit time.
2. **What is PDR?** Received packets / transmitted packets × 100.
3. **What is end-to-end delay?** Time between packet send and receive across the network.
4. **What is packet loss?** Packets transmitted but not successfully received.
5. **Why vary one parameter at a time?** To make the effect of that parameter easier to interpret.
6. **What happens when offered load exceeds channel capacity?** Contention and packet loss may increase, and throughput may stop increasing proportionally.
